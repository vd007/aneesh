SAARTHI - EDITION 04 CLIENT VIEW

This package uses your attached Edition 04 design.

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Open SAARTHI-Edition-04-Client-View.html in your browser.
3. Go to Research & Intelligence > Research Reports in the sidebar.
4. Click Download Full Report (PDF) on a report.

Keep the assets and reports folders beside the HTML file.
No hosting or internet connection is required.

Includes the original Edition 04 pages and imagery, plus an annual report
showcase, searchable report library, contents previews and PDF downloads.
The four PDFs are illustrative samples, not published HDFC Capital research.
The official annual publication can replace the sample when available.
All request workflows remain simulated; saved items last for this session.
